Weight conversion between kilograms and pounds is one of the most common conversions needed in daily life. Whether you are tracking your fitness goals, following a recipe, shipping packages, or traveling internationally, knowing how to convert between weight units is essential.

## The Basic Conversion

The fundamental relationship: **1 kilogram = 2.20462 pounds**

For quick mental math, remember that 1 kg is roughly 2.2 lbs. This approximation is accurate enough for most everyday situations.

## Smaller Units

For smaller weights:
- 1 ounce (oz) = 28.35 grams
- 1 pound = 16 ounces = 453.6 grams
- 1 gram = 0.0353 ounces

## The Stone Unit

In the UK and Ireland, body weight is often expressed in stones. One stone equals 14 pounds or approximately 6.35 kilograms. So someone weighing 70 kg would say they weigh 11 stone.

## Tips for Quick Conversion

1. **kg to lbs**: Double the kg, then add 10% of the result. Example: 70 kg → 140 + 14 = 154 lbs (actual: 154.3)
2. **lbs to kg**: Halve the pounds, then subtract 10% of the result. Example: 150 lbs → 75 - 7.5 = 67.5 kg (actual: 68.0)
