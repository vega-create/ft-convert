Whether you are buying property, planning a garden, or comparing living spaces internationally, understanding area measurements is crucial. Different countries and industries use different units, which can be confusing.

## Common Area Units

### Square Feet (ft²)
The standard unit for property measurement in the US, Canada, and parts of Asia. A typical American apartment might be 700-1,200 square feet.

### Square Meters (m²)
Used in most countries outside North America. A typical European apartment might be 50-100 square meters. One square meter equals about 10.76 square feet.

### Acres
Used for larger land areas in the US and UK. One acre is 43,560 square feet or about 4,047 square meters. A football field is about 1.32 acres.

### Hectares
The metric equivalent for large land areas, widely used internationally. One hectare is 10,000 square meters or about 2.47 acres.

## Quick Conversions

- 1 m² = 10.764 ft²
- 1 ft² = 0.0929 m²
- 1 acre = 43,560 ft² = 4,047 m²
- 1 hectare = 10,000 m² = 2.471 acres
- 1 km² = 100 hectares = 247.1 acres

## Practical Size References

- Parking space: ~150 ft² (14 m²)
- Studio apartment: ~400-600 ft² (37-56 m²)
- Average US home: ~2,300 ft² (214 m²)
- Tennis court: ~2,808 ft² (261 m²)
- Soccer field: ~1.76 acres (0.71 hectares)
- City block: ~2-5 acres (0.8-2 hectares)
